# Client logo sourcing — batch A

Reviewed on 2 November 2026. Scope: the first 90 printed profile names, plus priority SAPTCO and Lödige Industries.

**Delivered: 34 new, distinct, authentic corporate marks** in `content/client-logos-a.json` and `public/clients/verified-a/`. Existing eight logos and their manifest were not changed. No generated marks, text-only logo imitations, stock imagery, acronym guesses, or duplicates were added to fill a numeric target.

The relationship source remains the owner-supplied November 2025 AKL profile (pages 8–9). Current brand artwork does not independently verify a live commercial relationship, recruitment volume, or endorsement. Rebrands are explicitly labelled; source spelling is retained in `profileName`.

## Validation

- All 34 files decode and have nonzero intrinsic dimensions; all are under 150,000 bytes. Total batch weight: 1,154,323 bytes.
- Five SVGs were parsed with external entity resolution disabled, and checked for scripts, event handlers, foreign objects, external links/images and CSS external URLs. No such content remains.
- Raster images were optimized and, when useful, resized for website use. Blank outer margins were trimmed on eight assets without altering brand artwork or colors.
- A contact sheet compared every mark on light and dark surfaces. Dark plates are required for Al Mana, Agility, Ontegra, Bin Taleb and the plain white Abeer mark. All remaining batch-A plates are light.
- Abeer uses the plain official white brand mark; a dated 25-year anniversary badge and an alternative medical-centers legal-caption version were excluded.
- Aliases are counted once: AL EWAN / ALEWAN; AL MEER / ALMEER. No farm/holding-company duplication.

- Ansab and Emerging Energy: company-uploaded artwork on their verified Saudi Contractors Authority profiles is used because the official corporate sites reject automated retrieval.
