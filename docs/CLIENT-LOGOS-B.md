## Verification and preparation

- `profileName` and `profilePage` are copied exactly from `content/profile-extracted.json`, extracted from the owner-supplied AKL company profile. These records are the relationship evidence; public company websites establish the company identity and artwork source.
- Logos come from the named company’s official site or its clearly linked group/parent page. Group-brand use is explicitly disclosed in each manifest note. This does not create a second partner for each subsidiary sharing one mark.
- QSS is an unmodified inline header SVG extracted from its official website. Its `assetUrl` is the containing page. Seder is the actual emblem in its site’s declared Apple touch icon. Modern Bus uses its specific subsidiary mark from its parent Nesma’s official page.
- Raster artwork was proportionally resized only when necessary, converted to optimized PNG and kept below800×400px. Artwork was not regenerated, recolored, redrawn, or cropped. Original SVG artwork is retained.
- All SVG files parse successfully; no scripts, event-handler attributes, foreign objects, external references, document/entity declarations, or imported styles are present.
- Two final contact sheets were visually inspected. Dark tiles are required for JAL Human Resources, Saudi Medical Systems, Wooden Bakery, and United Catering. Other marks use a light tile.
- Naser Al Sayer’s original official mark is81×75px. Keep this mark in the smaller wall tiles; no upscaled fabricated replacement was created.
